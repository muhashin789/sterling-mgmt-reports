2 · Standards & weights

Score for each measure = MIN(100, Actual ÷ Standard × 100) — meeting the standard gives 100, nobody gets more. Quality is reversed because a lower DHU is better. Weights add to 100%, so the total is out of 100. Grade: A ≥ 85 · B 75–84.9 · C 65–74.9 · D < 65. Ranked only with + line-days.
